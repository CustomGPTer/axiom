The algebra engine

Roots, trigonometry, logarithms, exact forms and algebra use Symja (matheclipse-core, parser and external), copyright the Symja authors, under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 3. Axiom uses the library unmodified. Because Axiom's own source is published, the library can be replaced with a version of your choosing and the app rebuilt. The licence text is at gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-3.0.html.
