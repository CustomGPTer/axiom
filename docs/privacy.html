Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026. This policy covers the Axiom app for Android (package uk.jj.axiom) and this website. Axiom is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom.

The short version

Axiom collects nothing. It has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. Everything you type stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your history, memories, saved functions, the sums and lists you were last working on, and your settings are kept in the app's own private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it.

What the app can reach

Axiom asks Android for no permissions. It cannot use the internet, your files, your camera, your microphone, your location or your contacts.

It copies an answer to the clipboard when you hold it. It does not read the clipboard.

Services the app contacts

None. There are no adverts, no analytics, no crash reporting and no update checks.

Backups

A backup file is made only when you ask for one in Settings, and is saved where you choose through Android's own file picker. It holds your history, memories, functions and settings as plain text. Anyone who has the file can read it, so keep it somewhere you trust. Android's own device backup may also include the app's data if you have that turned on in your phone's settings.

Deleting your data

History can be cleared from the History screen, and memories and functions from the letters sheet. Uninstalling Axiom removes everything it stored. Backup files you saved are yours to delete.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and has no tracking or analytics of its own. GitHub, as the host, receives the technical details any web server receives, such as your IP address; see GitHub's privacy statement. The download button fetches the app file from GitHub.

Children

Axiom is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no information about you, there is nothing to ask for, correct or delete. Everything is on your own phone and under your control.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on GitHub. This policy was last updated on 21 November 2026.
