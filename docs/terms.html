What Axiom is

Axiom is a calculator. It is checked with care: see the home page for how. It is still software, and software can be wrong.

Checking important results

Do not rely on Axiom alone for a result that safety, health, money, an exam mark or a legal duty depends on. Check such results by a second, independent method. In particular, structural, electrical, medical and financial calculations must be checked by a suitably qualified person.

Graph readings and triangle-solver answers are rounded. The constants are published recommended values and the formula sheets are a reminder, not a textbook.
